Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server? +
No. FitFrame is 100% client-side. All image processing happens directly in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and instant processing speeds.
Can I use this for client proofing? +
Yes! Click "Export Contact Sheet" to generate a clean PDF containing all your uploaded photos and their file names. Send this PDF to your clients for fast approval before you export the final resized images.
How does the "Target < 1MB" feature work? +
Social media platforms compress images over 1MB, which ruins quality. If you toggle this on, FitFrame uses a binary search algorithm to automatically compress your image until it is safely under 1MB without visibly degrading quality.
